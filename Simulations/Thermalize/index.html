What is real here

Thermal cross sections are measured values (Sears, 1992, as published by NIST). How each one changes with energy is a model: the 1/v law, the best-known resonances, and a fast value. Right shape, right size, not an evaluated library. Neutron speeds on screen are compressed about 11:1 where the true range is 9,000:1, and the bench is flat, so nothing here is a critical mass.
