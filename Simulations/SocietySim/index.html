Conditions
Land regrowth—

How fast a grazed field comes back. This, more than anything else, sets how many people the world can hold.

Harvest rate—

What one farmer can lift from a field in a day. Raising it does not create food — it just strips the land faster.

Storage efficiency—

How much of a surplus survives being put by. Cheap storage is what lets wealth accumulate at all.

Heritable drift—

How far a child's metabolism strays from its parent's. At zero, nothing evolves.

Who's who

Colour is stored capital, not food. A farmer who is going hungry today shows red whatever they have in the granary.
